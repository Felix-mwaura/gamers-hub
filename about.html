Built by gamers, in Nairobi

We started Gamers Hub because buying games in Kenya used to mean guessing whether a link was real, waiting three days for a reply, and hoping the code worked.

Our story

Gamers Hub began in 2019 as a WhatsApp group trading second-hand controllers between classmates. It turned into a shop on Kimathi Street, then into this store — but the principle has not changed: treat people the way you would want to be treated when you are dropping a month's savings on a console.

Today we stock PS5 and Xbox titles, consoles, controllers, headsets, gaming phones and tablets. Everything is genuine and region-free, everything physical carries a local warranty, and every digital code is delivered the moment your M-Pesa payment clears.

What we stand for

No fakes, ever

Pirated keys, grey-market consoles and "refurbished" phones sold as new are how stores lose customers. We only stock licensed goods, and we say so on every listing.

Honest advice

If a cheaper option suits you better, we will tell you. If a game is not worth it at full price, we will say that too. Repeat customers beat one-off markups.

Fast, tracked delivery

Same-day in Nairobi, 1–3 days nationwide, and a WhatsApp update at every step. You should never have to wonder where your parcel is.

How we work

  • Sourcing: we buy direct from distributors and publishers where possible, which is why our prices hold steady instead of swinging with the parallel market.
  • Testing: every console and phone is bench-tested for at least 24 hours before it goes on sale.
  • Warranty: 12 months on hardware, 6 months on accessories, handled in-store — not shipped back to Dubai.
  • Trade-ins: bring your old gear in and we will value it against anything on the shelf.

Come say hello

The shop is on Kimathi Street in Nairobi CBD, open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 8pm. There is usually someone playing something on the demo TV. Pop in, or message us on WhatsApp if that is easier.
